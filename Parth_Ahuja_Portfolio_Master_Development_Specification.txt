1. Product Vision and Positioning

The site should communicate a coherent intersection of business analytics, product thinking, operations/supply-chain problem solving, software development and practical AI implementation. Positioning should be based on evidence that is actually published, not generic labels.

Primary experience promise

A visitor should be able to understand who Parth is in seconds, identify relevant work in under a minute, open proof without friction, and ask an AI assistant for the exact evidence they need.

Core jobs-to-be-done

• “Tell me who Parth is and what roles he is pursuing.”

• “Show me the strongest work, not an exhaustive list first.”

• “Let me verify a project through its live URL, GitHub, report, slides or video.”

• “Let me understand skills through evidence rather than self-rated percentages.”

• “Let me ask one question and jump directly to the supporting project or résumé section.”

• “Show me how AI is actually used in the portfolio itself.”

RAG pipeline

1. Write approved content into structured records/MDX.

2. Chunk semantically by project section, skill evidence, certificate, experience item and research finding.

3. Embed approved chunks and store metadata such as type, slug, visibility, version and source URL.

4. Classify incoming questions: project, skill, resume, experience, contact, navigation, role match, research or general.

5. Retrieve via semantic + keyword search with metadata filters.

6. Call structured tools where exact facts/URLs are needed.

7. Generate concise answer plus source links and next action.

8. Log privacy-safe quality metrics and review unanswered questions as content gaps.

14. Performance, SEO, Accessibility and Security

Performance

• Poster-first media; lazy-load project videos and avatar sessions.

• Optimize images with responsive sizes and modern formats.

• Keep AI/chat/3D code split from the recruiter path.

• Render core content server-side so it is usable with JavaScript disabled.

• Use CDN/object storage for large assets.

• Audit third-party scripts and load them only when necessary.

SEO / AEO

• Unique title/description/canonical per project and research page.

• Person, WebSite, CreativeWork/SoftwareApplication and BreadcrumbList schema where appropriate.

• XML sitemap and clean robots rules.

• Plain semantic text equivalents for interactive and AI content.

• Optional llms.txt / AI-readable profile manifest with explicit “do not infer” fields.

• Stable URLs such as /work/chat-with-pdf.

Accessibility

• WCAG 2.2 AA as the quality target.

• Keyboard access for navigation, AI, modals, filters and media.

• Visible focus states.

• Captions + transcript for every project video; text alternative for avatar.

• No information only by color, sound or motion.

• Reduced-motion and reduced-transparency support.

Security and privacy

• No AI/provider secrets in the browser.

• Auth-protected CMS/admin; role-based publishing/reindexing.

• Rate-limit AI/contact endpoints; add bot protection.

• Validate all inputs and external URLs.

• Treat retrieved text as untrusted data to reduce prompt-injection risk.

• Minimize chat retention and clearly disclose analytics/AI behavior.

• Whitelist asset types and size limits for CMS uploads.

Market interpretation

The strongest product direction is not “everything is AI”. It is a professional portfolio where AI is an acceleration layer: search, explanation, evidence discovery, project Q&A and optional voice/avatar. This matches the observed market direction toward agentic and AI-native interfaces while retaining human-verifiable proof.
